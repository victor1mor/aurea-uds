# PressableFeedback

A touch target that answers the finger. By default the whole piece shrinks a little while pressed (0.985 on a piece 300 wide, less on a wider one, in 300 ms); inside it, PressableFeedback.Highlight lights a layer, PressableFeedback.Ripple grows a wave from the finger, and PressableFeedback.Scale shrinks one child. It is a button for the screen reader, and one element: what goes inside is content, not another button.
