---
title: v0.25.0
description: Focus is drawn as a single line instead of border plus ring, and the selected tab is finally visible against its track.
date: 2026-10-08
---

Focus used to draw two lines on any part with a border: the part's own border, a gap, and the yellow ring. It is now a single line. The selected item in `Tabs` and `SegmentedControl` also gets its own surface and a small shadow, so it can be seen in both themes. No props changed.

## New

- **Token `--segment`**: the surface of the selected item on a track. Light: white; dark: `oklch(0.3964 0.01 285.93)`; `lory` brand in dark: `oklch(0.312 0.02 250)`.
- **Token `--shadow-sm`**: the small shadow under that surface, with three layers.
- **React Native: `t.shadowLayers`**: every shadow with all its layers, in the list format of React Native's `boxShadow` (for example `boxShadow: t.shadowLayers.shadowSm`). The token generator used to keep only the first layer of a shadow; `t.shadow` still has the first layer, so code reading `.offsetX` keeps working.

## Changed

- **Focus is one line (web).** Before, the focus ring sat 2px away from every part and also turned every border yellow with `!important`, giving border, gap and ring on 84 parts: fields, `Select`, `Textarea`, `InputGroup`, `Combobox`, drop zones, checkbox, switch, and outline, secondary and ghost buttons.
- **Parts with their own border**: the border takes the focus color and the ring touches it, a single 3px band (1 of border plus 2 of ring).
- **Parts without a border** (solid button, ghost button, tab, menu item): the ring stays offset, as before. A transparent border no longer turns yellow.
- **Checked checkbox and switch on**: the ring stays offset, because the fill has the ring's color and would disappear if touching.
- **Invalid field**: the ring takes the danger color, so yellow does not hide the red.
- **The selected tab is visible (web and React Native).** In [`Tabs`](./tabs.html) and [`SegmentedControl`](./segmentedcontrol.html) the selected capsule was `--secondary` on a `--muted` track: the same color in light (1:1) and 1.02:1 in dark. It is now `--segment` with `--shadow-sm`: white with a small shadow in light, a lighter gray in dark. The segmented control keeps the brand-colored text and its thin line. This also applies where the segmented control appears inside other parts (`ButtonGroup`, `ToolPermission`).
