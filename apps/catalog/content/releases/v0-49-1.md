Two layout fixes on native, and a clearer note on the `link` tone. Read "Look and feel" if you use `fullWidth` on native.

## Look and feel

- **A native `Button` with `fullWidth` changes height**: it now has its size's height — `sm` 40, `md` 48, `lg` 56 (`xs` 32, `xl` 64) — instead of 44 for every size. Screens with full-width buttons get shorter or taller; the touch target keeps its 44 minimum.

## Fixed

- **Native `Button` with `fullWidth`: the size's height only held for the regular button.** The box took `flex: 1` inside the touch target, and in a column that sets the base to zero: it ended up with the target's height. Now only the width changes, as the API says.
- **Native `Header`: the `brand` shrinks, and the actions do not.** With a long title on a narrow screen, the action on the right went off the screen. Now a one-line title ends in an ellipsis and the actions keep their size; with room to spare, nothing changes. On the web, the bar wraps to a second row below 1024 and never had the problem.
- **The `tone` note for `link` said the opposite of the current palette** — on `Text`, `Heading`, `Paragraph` and `Code`. `link` is for a real link only (the highlighter on the light theme, the yellow underlined on the dark one); for emphasis that is not a link, use `primary`.
