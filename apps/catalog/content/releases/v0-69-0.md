## Added

- **`direction` on the native `AureaProvider`** — `"ltr"` or `"rtl"`, the same prop the web provider already has. Unset, it follows the device setting (`I18nManager.isRTL`). A nested provider without it keeps the outer one.

## Changed

- **Arrows that point one way now point the way the text reads** when the provider says `direction="rtl"`. Left-to-right layouts do not change.
- Web: the `Breadcrumb` separator, the `Pagination` previous and next arrows, the `Carousel` previous and next buttons, the `NavList` row chevron, the submenu arrow in `DropdownMenu`, `ContextMenu` and `Menubar`, the `InterAgentMessage` arrow and the `DataGrid` detail button.
- Native: the `NavList` row chevron and the submenu arrow in `DropdownMenu` are mirrored. A custom `chevron` you pass to `NavList` is left as it is.
