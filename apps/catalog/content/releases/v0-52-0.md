New options on five web components. Nothing changes unless you use them.

## New

- **`AppShell topbarVariant="pill"`** — the top bar is a capsule, 64 tall and 14 from the top, as wide as the block. With `layout="docs"` the sidebar, the text and the table of contents start at 96, 28 apart; the table of contents is 236 wide and as tall as what it shows, and the sidebar ends 16 above the bottom of the window. Outside `"docs"`, the capsule runs edge to edge. **`topbarBlurred`** blurs what scrolls under the bar.
- **`Header blurred`** now works on `variant="pill"` too, and `maxWidth` on the pill makes the capsule itself that wide instead of hugging its content.
- **`TableOfContents progress`** — a reading ring in place of the heading icon: how much of the page has been read, in the ring and as a number. It is hidden from screen readers, which already hear the current section. On plain HTML pages, `aurea.js` keeps it up to date.
- **`TableOfContents indicator="segment"`** — the current item in a raised capsule, like the `Sidebar`'s. The default, `"line"`, is the stroke at the side.
- **`Accordion indicator="plus"`** — a plus in a 32 circle that turns into a yellow × when the item opens — and **`size="lg"`**, a 60-tall row with body-size text, 22 from the edge.

## Changed

- **Collapsible groups in `Sidebar`**: the arrow sits at the end of the group name, and the items are indented one step (28). On plain HTML pages, `aurea.js` opens and closes the group.
