A new sidebar variation, on the web. Nothing changes unless you turn it on.

## New

- **`Sidebar contrast`**, and **`AppShell sidebarContrast`**, which forwards it to the navigation sidebar. The sidebar carries the dark theme inside, like the contrast card: the text, the icons and the current item come from the brand's dark theme. Only the surface follows the page: on a light page it is the dark card; on a dark page it goes darker than the page, so the two still stand apart. The page that counts is the nearest themed ancestor — a dark band on a light page counts as dark. It works with every arrangement: floating, against the edge (`variant="flush"`), the rail and the panel; the boxless `plain` sidebar paints the surface when it is dark.
- **The `sidebar-contrast`, `sidebar-contrast-accent`, `sidebar-contrast-foreground` and `sidebar-contrast-muted` tokens, on the Ember brand only**, in both themes: Ember keeps its own measured colours for the dark sidebar. On every other brand the tokens do not exist, and the sidebar works the colours out from the dark theme.
