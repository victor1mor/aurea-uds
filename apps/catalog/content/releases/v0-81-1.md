## Changed

- **`@aurea-uds/contracts` is in English.** The contract now declares `"language": "en"`, and its rules (the hard rules, the media rules, the distribution and icon rules) are written in English. The rules themselves did not change.
- **The accessibility promise says what is measured.** The target is still WCAG 2.2 AA. The contract now also says that today the automated sweep checks the WCAG 2.0 and 2.1 A and AA rules, that the WCAG 2.2 rules are checked before 1.0, and where the known gaps are listed. Two new fields carry this: `universalCapabilities.accessibility.measuredToday` and `universalCapabilities.accessibility.knownGaps`.
- **Gradients are allowed.** The tokens file (`@aurea-uds/tokens/json`) no longer lists `no-gradients` among its principles.
- **The descriptions of the seven packages are in English**, as they appear on the npm registry.

No component, token value or style changes.
