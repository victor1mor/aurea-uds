---
title: v0.17.0
description: BottomNav no longer shifts when switching tabs, is shorter with labels closer to icons, and gains the capsule and expand indicators.
date: 2026-10-03
---

`BottomNav` was reworked on the web and in React Native. The bar no longer moves when the selected tab changes, it is shorter, and labels sit right under the icons. Two indicators are new: `capsule` and `expand`. Apps get the steadier, slimmer bar just by upgrading.

## New

- **`indicator="capsule"`**: a 56 × 32 yellow capsule behind the icon, present on every item and painted only on the selected one; the label sits below, outside it. The counter stays on the icon's corner and inverts on yellow (web), as in `circle-bold`.
- **`indicator="expand"`**: other items show only the icon; the selected one becomes a yellow capsule with the label beside it, at 14. The selected slot has a fixed width (136), so buttons move but the bar does not. Labels longer than 80 end with an ellipsis.
- **Hidden labels in `expand`**: labels of unselected items leave the screen but stay available to screen readers (`.sr-only` on the web, `accessibilityLabel` in React Native).

## Changed

- **A shorter [`BottomNav`](./bottomnav.html)** on both targets: 54 for `none`, `subtle`, `pill` and `expand`; 58 for `circle-bold`; 62 for `circle`, `circle-raised`, `circle-outline` and `capsule`, which have a 32 frame around the icon. Before, `circle-bold` measured 73.
- **Label size**: 12 on a 16 line (was 14 on 21 in React Native).
- **Spacing**: the button's top and bottom padding goes from `space1` to `space05`, and the label touches the icon (gap zero, was `space05`).
- **`circle-bold`**: the 32 frame is removed; the circle is the disc, 56 × 48.
- **Unselected labels** now use medium weight, the same as the selected one.

## Fixed

- **The bar moved when switching tabs** (web and React Native). The selected label got heavier, and heavier text is wider; in `circle-bold` the circle was the selected item itself, measuring 56 while the others measured their content. Now no size depends on selection, only the paint changes: every label has medium weight, and the `circle-bold` circle is a disc inside every item (`.bottom-nav-disc` on the web), yellow only when selected.
