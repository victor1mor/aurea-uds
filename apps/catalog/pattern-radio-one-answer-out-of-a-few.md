# One answer out of a few

Radios show every option at once, which is their whole advantage over a select — and their limit: past five or six the list stops being scannable and a select or combobox reads faster.

## Usage

```tsx
import {Radio} from "@aurea-uds/react";
```

```tsx
<fieldset>
  <legend>Density</legend>
  <Radio name="density" label="Compact" />
  <Radio name="density" label="Comfortable" defaultChecked />
  <Radio name="density" label="Spacious" />
</fieldset>
```
